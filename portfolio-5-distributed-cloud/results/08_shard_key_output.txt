Busiest state (Lagos) has 2.9x the orders of an average state:
whichever shard holds it becomes overloaded. And with only 18 possible key values,
the data can never be split into more than 18 pieces, however much the shop grows.

==================================================================
2026-10    1651 orders
2026-09    5400 orders
2026-08    5490 orders
Dates only ever increase, so EVERY new order lands on the shard holding "the latest dates".
One shard takes all the writes (a "hot shard") while the others sit idle.
